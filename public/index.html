ApplyAndFly

Your personal job-application assistant on WhatsApp.

ApplyAndFly watches your Gmail inbox for job-application confirmations, rejections, interview invitations, and offers, then keeps you updated over WhatsApp — no manual tracking spreadsheet needed. When a new application confirmation comes in, it automatically researches the hiring company and sends you a short, verified summary along with a link to your personal dashboard, where every application you've made is tracked in one place.

What it does

  • Reads your Gmail (read-only) to detect genuine application-related emails
  • Identifies the company and role, and researches the company from verified public sources
  • Sends you a WhatsApp notification with the summary and a link to your dashboard
  • Tracks every application's status (Applied, In Progress, Rejected, Hired) automatically as related emails arrive
Getting started: message the ApplyAndFly WhatsApp number to begin — you'll be guided through connecting your Google account.
