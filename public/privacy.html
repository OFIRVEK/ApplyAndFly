Privacy Policy — ApplyAndFly

Last updated: August 7, 2026

ApplyAndFly ("the app") is a personal job-application assistant. It reads your Gmail inbox to detect job-application confirmation emails, looks up public information about the hiring company, and sends you a summary over WhatsApp. This page explains what data the app accesses, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

Who runs this app

ApplyAndFly is operated by an individual developer for personal use, not a company. Contact for any privacy question or data request: ofirvqa@gmail.com.

What data the app accesses

  • Gmail (read-only). Using Google's gmail.readonly scope, the app reads the subject, sender, date, and body of messages already in your inbox to identify job-application confirmations, rejections, interview invitations, and offers. The app never sends, modifies, labels, forwards, or deletes any email, and never accesses attachments beyond the message text itself.
  • WhatsApp messages. Your WhatsApp phone number and the text of messages exchanged with the app, used solely to deliver notifications and handle setup (e.g. connecting your Google account).
  • Application data you generate. Company name, job title, application status, and a short company summary are stored so the app can show you a dashboard of your applications.

How your data is used

Email content is analyzed only to determine whether a message is a genuine job-application update and, if so, to extract the company name and position. Once a company is identified, the app searches public web sources to build a short company summary, then sends that summary to you over WhatsApp. Nothing is used for advertising, and your data is never sold.

Third-party services involved in processing

Because the app performs real-time research and delivers messages, the following third parties process data on the app's behalf:

  • Google (Gmail API) — provides read-only access to your inbox, per your explicit OAuth consent.
  • Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform — delivers and receives WhatsApp messages.
  • Groq (LLM inference) — receives the text of a candidate email to classify it and to summarize company research; does not receive your Gmail credentials.
  • Tavily (web search) — receives the company name and job details (not your email content) to find the company's official website and public information.
  • Firecrawl (web crawling) — reads the verified public website of a company you applied to, to gather accurate company details.

These services only receive the minimum information needed to perform their specific function (e.g. Tavily and Firecrawl never see your Gmail content; they only see company names and public web addresses).

Data storage and retention

  • Application tracking data (company, position, status, dates) is stored on the app's own server and is not shared publicly.
  • Google authentication is kept in memory for the running server process only and is not written to disk; it is cleared whenever the server restarts, requiring you to reconnect.
  • To request deletion of your data, email ofirvqa@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and state that you want your application-tracking data and any stored Gmail/WhatsApp identifiers removed. Requests are processed manually and completed within a reasonable time of receipt.

Your controls

  • Revoke Gmail access at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party access.
  • Stop WhatsApp notifications at any time by blocking the number or messaging the app to opt out.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at, and is not knowingly used by, children under 16.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
