ApplyAndFly

Your personal job-application assistant on WhatsApp.

What ApplyAndFly is

ApplyAndFly is a small, single-developer tool (not a company) built to solve one problem for its own operator: job hunting generates dozens of scattered emails — confirmations, rejections, interview invites, offers — that are tedious to track by hand in a spreadsheet. ApplyAndFly replaces that spreadsheet by watching your inbox for those emails and turning them into one always-up-to-date dashboard, with a WhatsApp message the moment something changes.

Purpose of app use

ApplyAndFly exists for one purpose: to automatically track your own job applications. It watches your Gmail inbox for job-application confirmations, rejections, interview invitations, and offers, then keeps you updated over WhatsApp — no manual tracking spreadsheet needed. When a new application confirmation comes in, it automatically researches the hiring company and sends you a short, verified summary along with a link to your personal dashboard, where every application you've made is tracked in one place.

Nothing else happens with your Gmail access: it is never used for anything beyond detecting and summarizing your own job-application emails, described in full below and in the Privacy Policy.

How this app uses Google user data

ApplyAndFly uses the Gmail API with the read-only scope (gmail.readonly), granted by you through Google's consent screen. Specifically, it:

  • Reads the subject, sender, date, and body of messages in your inbox — solely to identify emails related to your own job applications (confirmations, rejections, interview invitations, offers).
  • Never sends, modifies, labels, forwards, or deletes any email, and never accesses attachments.
  • Uses what it finds only to build your own application dashboard and WhatsApp notifications — your data is never shown to anyone else, never used for advertising, and never sold.
  • You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account security settings.

ApplyAndFly's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

What it does

  • Reads your Gmail (read-only) to detect genuine application-related emails, distinguishing them from unrelated mail, marketing/recommendation emails, and non-job "application" emails (e.g. a membership sign-up)
  • Identifies the hiring company and job title from the email, and researches the company from verified public sources (its own official website and public search results — never scraped from private sources)
  • Sends you a WhatsApp notification with a short summary of the company and role, plus a link to your personal dashboard
  • Tracks every application's status (Applied, In Progress, Rejected, Hired) automatically as related follow-up emails (rejections, interview invites, offers) arrive in your inbox
  • Gives you a web dashboard, reachable only via your own private link, listing every tracked application with its status, company summary, and applied date — with the ability to filter, sort, and manually correct a status
Getting started: message the ApplyAndFly WhatsApp number to begin — you'll be guided through connecting your Google account.
